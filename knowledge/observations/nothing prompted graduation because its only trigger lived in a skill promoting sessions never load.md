Asked what prompts graduating an eligible question, the session found one mechanism: a one-line awareness offer in the receiving graph's own generated skill. That skill loads only in sessions working inside that graph, while promotions run from source-graph sessions, so the offer never fired. The superseded spec had planned a linter to flag eligibility, and it went with the validator. 72 of the commons' 141 open questions had evidence from two or more domains, against 4 principles. That met the deferral condition for building the trigger: a silent, compounding gap.

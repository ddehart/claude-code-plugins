The commons listed 78 entries in its claims map against 81 files on disk. Every promotion run's redundancy
screen reads that map, so the three unlisted claims were invisible to all of them — and one of the three was
the claim closest to a candidate being promoted in that very run, recalled from memory rather than from the
index. A second map was missing two source notes including the previous run's. Neither gap was introduced by
the run that found them; neither had ever been detected, because a screen over an incomplete index returns
the same shape of answer as a screen over a complete one.

A redaction gate is deliberately two-part: a deterministic scan plus an agent read-through, because the
second half is judgment by design and patterns miss meaning. On its first real use the read-through
recommended **withhold**, citing 46 mentions of an employer's name and 20 of another project — counts
from `grep -c`, matched against a keyword in a "never capture" rule, without reading what the references
said.

One question reversed it: *"Is there anything truly sensitive related to any of those references, or are
they just references to the repo names?"* Reading the lines showed no employer content quoted, only a
four-stage shape the merged spec already described, and the names already present in four other files in
the same public repo.

Had the recommendation stood, the first exercise of the preserve stage would have been a withhold on
evidence that did not support it — false conservatism wearing the shape of diligence. The session's own
framing: *"judgment exercised on a tally produces confident answers about the tally."* The gate's second
half had silently become a second copy of its first.

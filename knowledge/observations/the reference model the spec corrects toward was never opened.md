A spec's central claim — that the pipeline is missing two of the four stages of the model it was built from
— rests entirely on the user's spoken account of that model. A search for the reference artifact ran early
in the session, returned two transcripts of assessments made against it, and the session proceeded without
comment; the 333 KB transcript sat on disk unread through twelve decisions, an independent cold read, and
five commits. The account is very likely accurate, since the same person authored both. That is exactly why
it went unchecked: a coherent, authoritative retelling supplies no felt reason to open the thing being
retold, and the gap leaves no trace in the artifact.

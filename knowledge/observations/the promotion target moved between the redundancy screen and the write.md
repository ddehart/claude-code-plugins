A promotion run screened four candidates against the commons for redundancy, then re-fetched before writing
because the skill mandates it. HEAD had moved — `18e4f55` to `a7ae33e` — while both fetches reported
"Already up to date," because the incoming commit had landed locally rather than upstream. Three of the four
candidates would have been associated wrongly. One would have been filed as corroboration of a claim it
actually contradicts, and the arriving material forced the retraction of a conclusion committed forty
minutes earlier. The best outcome of the run also came from that refresh: a candidate moved from a weak
corroboration to the third domain on an open cross-domain question. The screen and the write are separate
moments, and the gap between them is where a second writer lives.

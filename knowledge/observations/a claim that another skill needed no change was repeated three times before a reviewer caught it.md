After reading only one of a dependent skill's two prompts, the author reported that skill needed no wording change. It said so to the peer session, again in a later update, and once more in the spec's compatibility section. A reuse reviewer, briefed on a different question, found the other prompt still scoped to the old anchor. The correction had to go back to every place the claim had reached: "I told you twice that it didn't, and that was wrong."

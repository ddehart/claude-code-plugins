One inversion was claimed to explain everything downstream of it — a primary/fallback ordering, a guard
section, a survival check, a decision note — with the conclusion that "none of that would exist" otherwise.
The claim was assembled by reading the current state of those artifacts. No git history was consulted, no
blame run, none of the sessions that introduced them read. In the same document one provenance claim *was*
verified, by finding the amendment note that named the omission in writing, and it is conspicuously the
better-evidenced one. Some of that machinery may have had independent justification. The single-root story
is the most satisfying explanation available, produced by the party it exonerates.

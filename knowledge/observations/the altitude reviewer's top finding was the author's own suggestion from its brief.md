When the anchor problem first came up, the author put three options to Derek (a list of exact headings, twin deltas, or one heading) and he picked the list. Title matching was not on the menu. Later, the author's brief to an altitude reviewer asked whether "anchoring on heading title without the number" would be simpler and still exact. The reviewer returned it as its top finding, and the author presented it to Derek as the reviewer's, after which he chose to switch. The design held: title matching resolved once in every one of 13 live graphs. But the reviewer's agreement was the brief's own hypothesis coming back, not independent corroboration, and Derek's earlier pick had endorsed the best option on a menu that left the eventual answer off.

The harness's checks were edited five times after grading live runs. One required a real execution rather than a mention, one accepted a script run anywhere after the write, one bound model labels to the case's workspace, one compared notes line by line, and one voided only a denied execution. Four verdicts flipped to pass. The report listed each change with its reason and flagged two as judgment calls worth a skeptical look. The checker's self-test was re-run after the last edit.

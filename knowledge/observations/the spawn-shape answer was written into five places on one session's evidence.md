Within a single run, a mechanism for why delegated readers go silent was written into an observation note, a
question note marked "answered, ready to graduate," a map annotation, a changelog entry, and a task
instructing that a superseding delta be authored. The evidence was one named agent silent against five
unnamed reporting, in one session. A claim arriving from another domain the same day reported 43 of 44
workers silent across four briefs and two task shapes, which the mechanism cannot explain. Retraction cost
four file edits plus a task rewrite plus a follow-up commit. The speed of propagation and the cost of
reversal are the same property: restating a claim is cheap and produces something citable, so a thin
conclusion acquires the surface area of a settled one before anyone tests it.

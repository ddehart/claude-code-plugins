The spec's author ran the prescribed cold-read audit on its own draft, reported four gaps a fresh session
would hit, patched them, and committed. An independent reader on the same document then found four more,
including the one that would have propagated the defect into every future graph. Both audits asked the same
question of the same artifact; only the vantage differed. The self-audit is not weaker because it is less
careful — it audits the author's *intent*, and intent is precisely what is intact when the document
misstates it.

# a check keyed to the template's literal surface fails copies that diverged on purpose

A test, anchor, or match rule written while looking at the template reads the template's incidental
surface as part of the requirement: its stage numbers, its exact marker string, the cases it happens to
cover. Copies that were sharpened on purpose differ on exactly that surface, so the check fails them, and
because a failing check is re-offered on every run, the failure doesn't close. Key the check on the
invariant instead: the title rather than the numbered heading, what a record does rather than how it is
spelled, the case a copy covers rather than the cases the template covers. An enumeration of the
variants is not the fix: it misses the next one.

*One witness (2026-11-08, four instances in one session). Open.*

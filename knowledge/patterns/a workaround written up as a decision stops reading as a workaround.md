# a workaround written up as a decision stops reading as a workaround

A constraint forces a compromise. Someone writes the compromise down — in a skill, a decision note, a
conventions doc — with reasoning attached. From then on it reads as a choice that was made rather than a
shape that was forced, and the constraint that produced it becomes invisible.

The tell is prose that states a **correct premise and draws the opposite conclusion from it**. That
combination is what makes the write-up durable: it is locally reasonable, so a reader auditing it
top-to-bottom finds nothing wrong. Only someone asking why the arrangement exists at all recovers the
original constraint.

Distinct from ordinary staleness. A stale note is out of date; this one was never right, and the writing-up
is what protected it.

*Two witnesses, one discovery (2026-07-26): the generated knowledge-graph skill's synthesis line, and a
decision note dated 2026-07-22. Separate authoring events, found together — so independent as instances of
the shape, correlated as evidence that it recurs. Open.*

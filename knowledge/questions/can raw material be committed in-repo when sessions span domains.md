## question
Two of this project's settled principles are jointly unsatisfiable over the material it actually
processes, and the collision showed up on the first real run rather than at some margin.

**D2** says raw material is committed in-repo, because a durable location outside the repo reintroduces
the dangling pointer through a different door. **Never Capture** says other-domain particulars never
enter this public repo, because only plugin-craft lessons belong here and only in generalized form.

A session transcript is raw material that routinely names other domains, because the work spans them. So
"archive every transcript here" cannot be a standing rule, and neither principle is the one to abandon.

The first case resolved as *archive as-is* on the evidence — no employer content quoted, names already
public in four files in the same repo. That settled the instance and not the rule. What is still open:
is the resolution per-run judgment (the gate's read-through, every time), a policy (transcripts archive
only when they pass a stated test), or a change to where the archive lives for sessions that span domains?

Worth noting the failure mode observed while resolving it: the read-through reached for counts and a
keyword match first, and recommended withholding on evidence that did not support it. Whatever policy
lands, it needs to be one a tired reader can apply without reducing it to a tally.
